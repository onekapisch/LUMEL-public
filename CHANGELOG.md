# Changelog

All notable changes to LUMEL are documented here. Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/); releases are date-based and tied to the Mac App Store.

## [1.0] — 2026-06-20

First public release. **[Download on the Mac App Store ›](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/lumel/id6776136831)**

### Added
- **Boost mode** — brighten a supported XDR display beyond the macOS cap, toward its ~1600-nit peak.
- **Eclipse mode** — dim the display below the macOS minimum for dark rooms and night use (free, unlimited).
- **Compare mode** — preview boost on the left half of the screen (free).
- **Global shortcut** — toggle boost from any app with ⌃⌥⌘B, with no Accessibility permission required.
- **Battery-aware safeguards** — auto-off below a configurable 10–50% threshold; disabled in Low Power Mode.
- **Optional auto-off timer** — 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours.
- **Per-display control** — on/off per screen, remembered across reconnects and sleep.
- **LUMEL Pro** — one-time $2.99 in-app purchase unlocking full-screen boost across every display.
- Private by architecture: sandboxed, no network entitlement, collects nothing.
